# Biftu Leather Goods showroom brief

> Fictional disposable client used to exercise the SuqPage design workflow. All copy and imagery are demonstration material, not verified business claims.

## Identity

- Business name: Biftu Leather Goods
- Permanent handle: `demo-biftu-leather-goods`
- Location: Addis Ababa, Addis Ababa
- Existing logo/mark: generated geometric mark based on a B made from two stitched pocket curves
- Short description: Make the bags feel durable and modern without a dark brown luxury site.

## Customer And Goal

- Primary customer: working adults and practical gift buyers
- Main visitor decision: identify the closest offering and decide what requirement to send
- Inquiry outcome: one direct SuqPage inquiry with optional quantity and practical context
- Product, production, or custom-work mode: mixed as recorded per offering
- Information that must appear early: what the business makes or does, who it serves, and the four available starting points

## Brand Direction

- Customer color request: brown and black
- Designer advice: Leather already provides brown; indigo and berry create a contemporary interface while black is reserved for the inverse close.
- Logo concept: a B made from two stitched pocket curves
- Visual thesis: Construction close-ups framed by bold, cool editorial color.
- Visual directions to avoid: one-color tinting, generic stock scale, framed hero boxes, decorative clutter, unsupported claims

## Composition Direction

- Foundation choice: `artisan-clay`
- Foundation reason: artisan-clay supplies the typography, spacing, geometry, and media behavior because the brief fits material content anatomy; the palette is independently authored from the brand direction.
- Header and hero needs: compact identity, immediate offer, and integrated wide media with protected copy space
- Story/process distinction: opposing alignment and contrasting semantic surfaces without divider clutter
- Catalog density and media behavior: exactly four comparable cards with bounded 4:3 media
- Inquiry CTA and floating control: persistent but clear of phone content
- Footer needs: identity and contact close without category navigation

## Booth Direction

- Approved logo/name treatment: generated mark plus exact business name
- Facade material and color: shared clean City Suq architecture with the final primary and secondary roles
- Product/craft cue: a B made from two stitched pocket curves
- Generated image authorized: yes, for disposable demonstration use

## Acceptance Notes

- Desktop priorities: integrated hero, clean section rhythm, visible product comparison, decisive CTA
- Phone priorities: no horizontal overflow, readable logo, 44px actions, unobstructed inquiry control
- Accessibility/localization risks: long business names, custom palette contrast, reduced motion
- Client review questions: replace every provisional fact and illustration before any real-client publication
